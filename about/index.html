About

Know what your work is really worth.

WorthMyTime is being built for UK workers who want a clearer view of how money, working time, benefits and work-related costs fit together.

Why this site exists

A salary number can be useful, but it can also hide the reality of long hours, unpaid overtime, expensive commuting, weak benefits or a change in work-life balance. WorthMyTime focuses on those practical questions rather than acting as a generic salary calculator.

What WorthMyTime will cover

The site will grow into calculators and plain-English guides for true hourly rate, job offer comparison, overtime, pay rises, reduced hours, commuting, annual leave, pension value, bonuses, shift allowances and total compensation.

What it will not do

WorthMyTime does not provide personalised financial advice. Its tools are intended to support understanding and decision-making, not to tell you what job, contract or financial choice is right for you.
