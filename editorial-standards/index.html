Editorial standards

Plain-English guidance without overclaiming.

WorthMyTime content should be practical, UK-specific and clear about what a calculator can and cannot tell you.

Our principles

  • Use plain English and explain financial terms where they matter.
  • Distinguish objective calculations from judgement calls.
  • Avoid fake reviews, fake testimonials, invented usage statistics and unsupported claims.
  • Keep UK context visible when discussing tax, work patterns, benefits or commuting.
  • Update calculation assumptions when relevant rules change.

Responsible presentation

WorthMyTime should help people ask better questions about work. It should not pressure users into a decision or imply that a calculator result is the only valid way to value a job.
