Methodology

Calculations can be factual. Worth is personal.

WorthMyTime is designed to make assumptions visible and to avoid pretending that one hourly rate can answer every work decision.

Calculation before interpretation

Where possible, future tools will separate factual inputs and calculations from the interpretation of whether a job, offer or change feels worthwhile. A result can show a rate or comparison; it cannot know your priorities, health, caring responsibilities, career goals or tolerance for travel and stress.

Multiple useful measures

  • Headline hourly equivalent: pay divided by contracted working hours.
  • Net hourly rate: a take-home view after relevant deductions.
  • Time-committed rate: work time plus required travel or other work-related time.
  • Spendable work rate: income after work-related costs.
  • Total compensation rate: salary and employer-provided benefits considered together.

Assumptions and limitations

When calculators are added, assumptions should be stated near the result. Tax, pension, student-loan and benefit rules can change, and personal circumstances matter. WorthMyTime will avoid presenting outputs as personalised financial advice.
